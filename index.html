Why FarmLink?

Traditional agricultural supply chains leave farmers vulnerable to distress sales and dealers to unpredictable quality. FarmLink solves this with systemic transparency.

Direct Farmer-Dealer Trade

Eliminate unnecessary intermediaries and hefty commission cuts. Trade directly with licensed bulk dealers.

Fairer Price Discovery

Admin-verified APMC mandi reference bands guide bids, preventing below-cost distress sales.

Quality Transparency

Transparent Grade A, B, and C parameters clarify produce grade beforehand, stopping last-minute deductions.

Weather-Smart Advisories

Rule-based agricultural weather alerts advise farmers when to delay spray or harvest, saving input costs.

Core Innovation

Eliminating Price vs Quality Disputes

In traditional markets, a farmer may ask for ₹100 while a dealer claims low quality and forces ₹90. FarmLink removes ambiguity through structured quality criteria and official mandi price bands.

Grade A: High-grade uniform harvest, prime export quality, zero blemishes.
Grade B: Standard mandi quality, minor natural variances, standard market rate.
Grade C: Bulk processing grade for sauce, pulp, or milling.
